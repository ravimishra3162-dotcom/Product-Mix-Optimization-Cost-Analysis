📦**Product Mix Optimization for Computer Assembly & Testing Using Excel Analytics**

**🔹 Project Overview**

This project analyzes a computer manufacturing product mix problem using Excel-based operational data to optimize production decisions, evaluate labor costs, and improve overall operational efficiency. The analysis focuses on assembly and testing processes, labor-hour costs, and input constraints to support data-driven managerial decision-making.

The project demonstrates practical skills in operations analytics, cost modeling, and decision optimization, which are highly relevant to Data Analyst, Operations Analyst, and Project Manager roles.

🔹 **Business Problem**

A computer manufacturing unit must make informed production decisions while operating under limited labor resources and cost constraints. Management needs to understand how labor allocation affects total production cost and profitability.

The key business challenges include:

Understanding how assembly and testing labor costs impact total unit cost

Optimizing the product mix under limited labor availability

Improving profitability while meeting operational constraints

Supporting strategic and operational decisions with quantitative analysis

🔹 Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation**

Transformed unstructured Excel data into clean analytical tables

Removed blank rows and standardized variable names

Structured inputs to support cost and profitability analysis

2. **Cost Analysis**
Calculated assembly and testing labor cost per unit

Estimated total unit cost, including labor and component costs

Compared cost contribution of assembly vs testing operations

3. **Product Mix Evaluation**

Analyzed labor hour requirements for each product type

Evaluated the impact of labor constraints on production decisions

Identified key cost drivers influencing profitability

4. **Decision Support Analysis**

Assessed trade-offs between labor allocation and unit profit

Developed KPIs and visualizations to support managerial decisions

Generated recommendations for an optimal product mix strategy

🔹 **Key Insights**

Testing operations contribute disproportionately to total labor cost compared to assembly

Products with higher testing requirements significantly reduce unit profitability

Optimizing labor allocation can improve margins without increasing production volume

Excel-based cost models are effective tools for operational decision-making in manufacturing environments

🔹 **Business Recommendations**

Reallocate labor resources toward lower-cost processes where operationally feasible

Monitor testing efficiency closely to control cost escalation

Use structured Excel cost models for routine production planning

Standardize operational data collection to enable faster and more accurate analysis

🔹 **Conclusion**

This project demonstrates how Excel-based analytics can be used to solve real-world manufacturing and operations problems. By transforming raw operational data into structured cost models and KPIs, the analysis provides clear visibility into labor cost drivers and product profitability.

The results highlight the importance of data-driven product mix decisions in improving operational efficiency and profitability. This approach enables management to make informed decisions regarding labor allocation, cost control, and production planning.

Overall, the project reflects the analytical, problem-solving, and decision-support responsibilities expected of Data Analysts and Project Managers working in operations-driven environments.
